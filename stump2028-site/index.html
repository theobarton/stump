Win the nomination. Pick a running mate. Survive October. Take the White House.

This is a simulation, not a forecast. The candidates are real people, picked from names widely discussed as possible 2028 contenders as of November 2026. Their in-game ratings, starting positions and everything that happens to them are made up for the game. None of it is a claim about anyone's record, character or plans. You can play either party. State baselines use the 2024 presidential margins. The primary calendar is a simplified projection.
